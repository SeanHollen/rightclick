Open link in new tab

Settings are saved automatically on this browser.

Behavior

Extension enabled
The default is on, including after a browser restart.
Open in background
Do not switch to each newly opened tab.
Tab position
Place the tab beside the source or at the end.
Link scope
External means links to a different origin only.

Exceptions

Leave same-page links alone
Do not open section anchors such as #comments.
Leave downloads alone
Links marked as downloads keep normal behavior.
Respect modified clicks
Ctrl/Cmd, Shift, and Alt clicks keep Chrome's native behavior.

Per-site rules

Exact domains and wildcards such as *.example.com are supported. Exact rules take priority.
DomainBehavior
Known browser limits: extensions cannot intercept clicks on Chrome internal pages, the Chrome Web Store, browser UI, or bookmarks. Script-only controls without a real URL may also be unavailable. The extension does not claim otherwise.
