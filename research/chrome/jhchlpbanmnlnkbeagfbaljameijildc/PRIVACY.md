# Privacy policy

**Open link in new tab** does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data, browsing history, link destinations, or usage analytics.

The extension accesses pages only to detect a user-initiated click on a link and open that destination in a new tab. The destination is passed locally between the extension's content script and its service worker; it is never sent over the network by the extension.

Preferences and per-site rules are stored only in `chrome.storage.local` on the user's browser. The extension contains no advertising, analytics, remote code, accounts, or third-party services.

Chrome does not allow the extension to run on browser-internal pages, the Chrome Web Store, browser UI, or bookmarks.

Last updated: August 5, 2026.
